Our Story

A little corner of
heaven on the mainland.

Heaven & Eve was sent down to build a playground in your mouth — the kind of spot the mainland deserved, equal parts great cup and good company.

Premium coffee pulled with care, fresh-pressed juices, and a kitchen turning out our best-selling burgers. No WiFi, by design — just good signage, warm wood, and a seat that says focus, or don't. Whether you're here to work, to meet, or just to slow down for ten quiet minutes, there's a chair with your name on it.
